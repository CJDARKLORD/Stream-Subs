─────────────── English ───────────────

Stream Subs — important before first launch

On first launch the app downloads about 4 GB:
  📦 Python packages (torch, faster-whisper) — ~2.5 GB
  🎤 Whisper "medium" model — ~1.5 GB
  🔍 Silero VAD model — ~2 MB

⏱  This takes 10-30 minutes depending on your internet speed.
❌ Do not close the bootstrap window during download.

✅ After the first setup, components are cached and later
launches are instant.
